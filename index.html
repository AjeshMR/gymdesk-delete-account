Delete Your GymDesk Account

You can request deletion of your GymDesk account and the personal data associated with it using the instructions below.

Important: Account deletion may be permanent. Please make sure you want to proceed before submitting your request.

Option 1: Request deletion online by email

If you cannot access your account or prefer not to use the app, contact our support team to request account deletion.

Include the email address registered with GymDesk so we can identify your account. We may ask you to verify your identity to protect your account from unauthorized deletion requests.

Request Account Deletion

Or email us directly at gymdesk.official@gmail.com .

Option 2: Delete through the app

  1. Open the GymDesk app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Settings or your account profile.
  4. Select the account deletion option, if available.
  5. Follow the instructions to confirm your request.

If you cannot find an account deletion option, use the email request method above.

What data is affected?

A verified deletion request will be handled according to GymDesk's data-deletion procedures and applicable requirements. This may include:

  • Your GymDesk account and authentication details.
  • Your profile and contact information.
  • Membership information associated with your account.
  • Other personal information associated with your account.

Certain records, such as transaction or payment records, may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping. Any retained data should be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the applicable retention period.

How are requests processed?

Our support team will review your request and may contact you to verify account ownership. We will process verified requests according to our deletion procedures and inform you if any data must be retained.

For more information about how GymDesk handles personal data, please read our Privacy Policy .
